✨ A LITTLE MESSAGE FOR YOU ✨

Dear Friend ❤️

En life-la ungalai oru friend-aa kidaichadhu enakku romba special. Namma share pannina sirippu, conversations, silly moments, memories — idhellam enakku eppovume precious. ✨

Namma eppovume daily-aa pesaama irukkalaam. Sometimes life busy-aa irukkalaam. Aana adhunaala namma friendship-oda value enakku koraiyaadhu.

Ungala pathi enakku pidicha vishayam enna-na, neenga neenga-aa irukkuradhu. Ungaloda own way-la irukkura kindness, care and character enakku romba pidikkum. ❤️

Namma friendship future-la eppadi change aanaalum, namma share pannina memories enakku eppovume special-aa dhaan irukkum.

Ungalukku eppovume enna kashtam vandhaalum — happy-aa irundhaalum, sad-aa irundhaalum, lonely-aa feel panninaalum, illa life romba heavy-aa irundhaalum...

Enakku oru call pannunga. 📞❤️

Naan ellathayum solve panna mudiyama irukkalaam... Aana ungala listen panna naan eppovume ready-aa iruppen. 🫂

Ungaloda life-la neenga deserve panra happiness-um peace-um kidaikkanum-nu genuinely wish panren. 🌸

Thank you for being a part of my life.
Thank you for all the memories.
And most importantly...

Thank you for being my friend. ❤️

Always stay happy.
Always keep smiling. ✨

With lots of love,
